Official Photo Guidelines for 2025 Exams

Using the correct photo dimensions is critical for your application. Below are the official requirements for major Indian government exams.

1. UPSC Civil Services (IAS/IPS)

  • Dimension: 3.5cm x 4.5cm.
  • Format: JPG only. Size must be between 20KB and 300KB.
  • Rules: The photo must be recent (within 10 days). White background is preferred. Glasses are allowed only if eyes are clearly visible (no glare).

2. SSC CGL & CHSL

  • Date on Photo: Yes, SSC strictly requires the Date of Photo (DOP) printed at the bottom. Use our tool to add this automatically.
  • Size: 20KB to 50KB.
  • Mistake to Avoid: Do not wear a cap or goggles. Both ears must be visible.

3. IBPS PO & Clerk (Banking)

  • Photo: 4.5cm x 3.5cm (20KB - 50KB).
  • Signature: Black ink on white paper (10KB - 20KB). NOT in Capital Letters.
  • Thumb Impression: 20KB - 50KB (Blue/Black ink).
